# Privacy Policy for Container Tracker (Shopify App)

**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026

Container Tracker has created this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and share information when you install and use the Container Tracker app on your Shopify store. By using this App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Store Information
When you install the App, we receive and store the following information about your store:
*   **Store Domain:** Your store's permanent myshopify.com domain. It identifies your account in our system.
*   **Store Name:** Used to personalize the user interface.
*   **Contact Email:** Set from your store email on first use. You can change it at any time on the App's Settings page. We use it only to contact you about your account and tracking problems.

### B. Tracking Data
To provide the tracking service, we store:
*   **Container Numbers and Carriers:** The containers you choose to track and their shipping lines.
*   **Order Links:** The IDs of the orders or draft orders you link a container to. We store the ID only, not the order contents.
*   **Tracking Events:** The milestones we collect from the carriers, and the events you add yourself (manually or by importing a screenshot).
*   **Plan Usage:** Usage counts for your plan (for example, how many containers you track and how many AI features you use).

### C. No Customer (Buyer) Data
The App does not request access scopes to your customers, orders or products. We **do not** read, collect or store data about your customers (buyers), the contents of your orders, or any payment data. Billing for paid plans is handled entirely by Shopify.

### D. Website Analytics
Our website (get-ctms.com) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. It records aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring website, the country and the browser type.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
*   **Service Provision:** To process container numbers and retrieve tracking status from shipping lines.
*   **Contextual Integration:** To show the tracking status of the containers linked to an order on the order and draft order pages in your Shopify admin.
*   **Authentication:** To verify, through Shopify session tokens, that requests come from your store.
*   **Plans and Limits:** To apply the limits of your plan. We read your current plan from Shopify.
*   **Improvement:** To monitor the performance of the App and fix technical issues.
*   **AI-Enhanced Features:** To provide optional summarization and screenshot import upon your specific request.

## 3. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We only share, transfer, or disclose your information to third parties in the following limited circumstances:

*   **Microsoft Azure:** Your store information and tracking data are stored on our backend infrastructure hosted on Microsoft Azure (North Europe region) to provide the core tracking service.
*   **Google Vertex AI / Gemini:** We transfer specific data (container history or user-provided screenshots) to Google’s enterprise AI services only when you explicitly trigger an AI feature (an AI summary or a screenshot import). This data is processed to provide the requested output and is not used to train global models.
*   **Shipping Carriers:** Container numbers are shared with public shipping line websites and APIs to retrieve current status updates.
*   **Shopify:** We read your store's plan status from Shopify to apply your plan.
*   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

## 4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, and we store data on secure servers provided by Microsoft Azure. We use industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your data in transit and at rest. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

We are committed to managing your data responsibly.

*   **While Installed:** Your store information and tracking history are stored for as long as the App is installed on your store.
*   **Uninstalling:** When you uninstall the App, we stop tracking your containers. If you reinstall the App before your data is deleted, tracking resumes.
*   **Automatic Deletion:** 48 hours after you uninstall the App, Shopify sends us a data deletion request for your store. We then delete all data associated with your store, within 30 days at the latest.
*   **Customer Data Requests:** Because we store no data about your customers, Shopify's customer data requests and customer deletion requests do not concern any data held by us.
*   **AI Processing Data:** Data sent to our AI service (summaries or screenshots) is not retained by the AI provider for model training and is not stored by us after the session is complete. The resulting tracking events you choose to save are stored with your tracking history.
*   **Export or Deletion on Request:** You have the right to request a copy of your data, or the deletion of your account and all associated data, at any time. To do so, please contact us at **support@get-ctms.com**. We will process data deletion requests within 3 business days.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
