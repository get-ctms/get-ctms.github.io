# Privacy Policy for CTMS Container Tracker (monday.com App)

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

Container Tracker has created this Privacy Policy to explain how we collect, use, and share information when you install and use the CTMS Container Tracker app on your monday.com account. By using this App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
When you install the App, monday.com tells us your **account ID**. It identifies your account in our system. We do **not** store your name, email address, or the names or emails of your team members. monday.com includes the installing user's name, email and country in the install notification; we use it only to verify the request and do not store it.

### B. Tracking Data
To provide the tracking service, we store:
*   **Container Numbers and Carriers:** The containers you track and their shipping lines. The App reads the container number (and, if you pick one, the carrier column) of the item on which the automation runs.
*   **Item Links:** The IDs of the boards and items a container belongs to, so we can update the right item. We store IDs only, not item names or other column values.
*   **Automation Subscriptions:** When you turn on the "When CTMS finds a new event" automation, monday.com gives us an address to notify your board; we store it with the board ID until you turn the automation off or the App's data is deleted.
*   **Tracking Events:** The milestones we collect from the carriers, and the events you add yourself (manually or by importing a screenshot).
*   **Plan Usage:** Your plan and usage counts (for example, how many containers you track and how many credits you use).

### C. Board Data We Write
The App writes the tracking status, ETA and, if you choose, the last event of a container into the columns you pick in the automation. It does not read or change other columns, boards, files, or users.

### D. Website Analytics
Our website (get-ctms.com) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. It records aggregated data such as the page visited, the referring website, the country and the browser type.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

*   **Service Provision:** To process container numbers and retrieve tracking status from shipping lines.
*   **Board Updates:** To write status and ETA to your items when a carrier reports something new.
*   **Authentication:** To verify, through tokens signed by monday.com, that requests come from your account. We do not store monday.com access tokens.
*   **Plans and Limits:** To apply the limits of your plan. We read your current plan from monday.com.
*   **Improvement:** To monitor the performance of the App and fix technical issues.
*   **Summaries and Screenshot Import:** To provide optional summaries and screenshot import upon your specific request.

## 3. Contacting You

We do not send you emails or other messages. We have no contact data for you unless you email us; we then use your email address only to answer you.

## 4. Data Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We only share, transfer, or disclose your information to third parties in the following limited circumstances:

*   **Microsoft Azure:** Your account ID and tracking data are stored on our backend infrastructure hosted on Microsoft Azure (North Europe region) to provide the core tracking service.
*   **Google Vertex AI / Gemini:** We transfer specific data (container history or user-provided screenshots) to Google's enterprise AI services only when you explicitly request a summary or a screenshot import. This data is processed to provide the requested output and is not used to train global models.
*   **Shipping Carriers:** Container numbers are shared with public shipping line websites and APIs to retrieve current status updates.
*   **monday.com:** We read your account's plan from monday.com and write tracking values to your board through the monday.com API. Billing for paid plans is handled entirely by monday.com.
*   **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).

## 5. Data Security

We store data on secure servers provided by Microsoft Azure. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (Azure Storage encryption, AES-256). App secrets are kept in protected application settings, not in our source code. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

*   **While Installed:** Your tracking data is stored for as long as the App is installed on your account.
*   **Uninstalling:** When you uninstall the App, we stop tracking your containers. If you install it again within 7 days, tracking resumes where it stopped.
*   **Automatic Deletion:** 7 days after you uninstall the App, we permanently delete all data associated with your account (tracking data, item links, automation subscriptions and plan usage), within 10 days of the uninstall at the latest. The automations on your boards stay in monday.com until you delete them.
*   **Logs:** Our technical logs hold request metadata (such as the account ID and container numbers) for troubleshooting and are deleted after 30 days.
*   **Summaries and Screenshots:** Data sent for a summary or a screenshot import is not retained by the AI provider for model training and is not stored by us after the request. The tracking events you choose to save are stored with your tracking history.
*   **Export or Deletion on Request:** You can request a copy of your data, or the deletion of your account and all associated data, at any time at **support@get-ctms.com**. We process deletion requests within 3 business days.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
