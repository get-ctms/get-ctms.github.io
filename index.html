Supported Carriers

Container Tracker checks these shipping lines automatically and adds new tracking events to your container's history as they happen:

  • MSC
  • Maersk
  • Hapag-Lloyd
  • CMA CGM
  • ONE (Ocean Network Express)
  • Yang Ming
  • Evergreen

Shipping with another carrier? Track the container manually: add events yourself, or paste a screenshot of the carrier's tracking page and AI extracts the events for you.

How It Works

  1. Install the Google Sheets add-on or the HubSpot app.
  2. Submit a container: enter the container number (e.g. MSCU1234567) and pick the carrier.
  3. Follow its journey: see the full event history, key milestones (Gate In, Loaded, Departed, Arrived, Discharged, Delivered) and an AI summary of where the container is now.
  4. Export container lists, histories and milestones to Google Sheets for reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which shipping lines can I track automatically?

MSC, Maersk, Hapag-Lloyd, CMA CGM, ONE (Ocean Network Express), Yang Ming and Evergreen. Containers from any other carrier can be tracked manually or imported from a screenshot.

Where do I find Container Tracker in Google Sheets?

Container Tracker is a Google Workspace add-on, so it is not in the Extensions menu. Open any spreadsheet and click the Container Tracker icon in the right-hand side panel. If the side panel is hidden, click the small arrow at the bottom right of the window. See the Google Sheets guide.

Does it work in HubSpot?

Yes. The HubSpot app adds a Container Tracking card to your Tickets, so each shipment is tracked on the ticket it belongs to. See the HubSpot guide.

How much does it cost?

Your first 10 containers are free, with no credit card needed. After that, Pro is $15/month and covers up to 20 containers a month plus 400 AI credits. Save with a 6-month or yearly subscription. Tracking more containers? Contact us for a plan that fits your volume. See Pricing.

How often is a container checked?

The first check runs right after you add a container, and your first update usually arrives in about a minute. After that, Container Tracker checks about every 6 hours while the container is moving, less often during quiet periods, until it is delivered.

What happens when I reach my 10 free containers?

Nothing is deleted. Your containers and their history stay available. You can keep tracking manually for free, or upgrade to keep new containers updating automatically.

Does "20 containers a month" mean 20 at a time?

It means 20 new automatically tracked containers per billing month. Containers you are already tracking keep updating.

Is my data safe?

We store only your email, your name and the container numbers you submit, on Microsoft Azure in the EU. The add-on only writes to the spreadsheet you are working in, and only when you click an export. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Open the add-on, go to My Profile and click Manage Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I keep stakeholders updated?

Open a container and use the AI summary for a plain-language status update, or export milestones to a sheet to compare many containers side by side.
